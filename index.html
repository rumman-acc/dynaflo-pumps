About Dynaflo

Dynaflo, Inc. was founded in 1998 in Reading, Pennsylvania to address a critical need in the medical equipment industry for smaller, quieter, and more efficient vacuum pumps and compressors. With 18 employees led by President & CEO Kevin Shaw, the company designs and manufactures all products on-site in Pennsylvania using high-quality, precision materials with a focus on safe, reliable performance.

Award-Winning Innovation: In 2008, Dynaflo's custom engineered 3000 Series radial diaphragm air compressor was named a finalist in Design News' "Build a Better Mousetrap" competition.

Every Dynaflo pump is designed, engineered, manufactured, inspected, assembled, and tested on-site in Pennsylvania, ensuring the highest standards of quality and performance. The company serves OEM manufacturers and engineers developing medical devices, air and gas monitoring equipment, industrial automation systems, semiconductor and biotech applications, and food and packaging systems.

Dynaflo manufactures several series of custom diaphragm pumps, each engineered for specific performance requirements. All pumps can be fully customized including motor type, performance specifications, materials, wiring, connectors, and mounting.
